Search Algorithms: Binary vs Linear

Understanding the Concepts

Linear Search

How it works: It checks each element one by one from the beginning until it finds the target.

Why use it? It is simple and works on any list, even if the items are completely scrambled and unsorted. However, for large lists, it is very slow (like a turtle).

Binary Search

How it works: It jumps to the middle of the list. If the target is smaller, it discards the right half. If larger, it discards the left half. It repeats this until found.

Why use it? It is incredibly fast because it eliminates half of the remaining possibilities in every step! But why must it be sorted? Because if the list isn't sorted, knowing the middle value doesn't tell you whether your target is to the left or right.
